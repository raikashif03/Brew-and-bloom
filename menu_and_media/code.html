How We Make the Perfect Pour-Over

The 5-step precision brewing standard

  1. 1
    Grind fresh single-origin beans Coarse sea salt consistency for maximum aromatic extraction.
  2. 2
    Rinse filter with 94°C water Removes paper taste while gently preheating the ceramic cone.
  3. 3
    The 45-second bloom pour 60ml of water releases trapped CO2 for balanced sweetness.
  4. 4
    Gentle circular infusion Slow concentric spiral spirals maintaining a consistent brew bed.
  5. 5
    Swirl and serve in warm ceramics Aerates the liquor, blending delicate botanical nuances.
